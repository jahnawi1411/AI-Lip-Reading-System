A QUICK INTRODUCTION
Start with a moment.
1. Choose a feature and source
Choose words + lip expressions to get both from one clip, with sound (audio transcription) or without sound (visual speech models). Choose lip expressions only if you just want a timeline of smiling lips, pouting lips, pursed lips, closed lips, slightly open lips and open mouth. Expression mode ignores audio and needs no microphone or speech model. These labels describe visible shapes, not emotions.
Upload a video or enable your webcam. For a webcam capture, press the button below the camera to start. Recording continues until you press Stop recording. Words and lip expressions are then shown together.
2. Review the result
Unknown characters and empty output are flagged as unreliable. Even readable candidates require review. Use English sentences for everyday English lip-reading, and review the words carefully. The first request loads the model; later requests reuse it. Research settings expose a restricted GRID command model and an unreliable original checkpoint for comparison only.
3. Keep or delete your history
Results are private to your account and persist between visits. Export your results or delete them individually. Video files and camera images are not retained.
Camera troubleshooting
Use HTTPS when deployed, or localhost on your PC. Allow camera permission and, for audio mode, microphone permission in the browser, close other apps using the camera, and keep this tab visible. If the camera is unavailable, use video upload.
About VisionSpeak
A visual speech recognition project using Python, TensorFlow, OpenCV, MediaPipe, Flask, HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The video + audio mode uses local Whisper speech transcription and a lip-shape timeline. Choose a visual model for silent video. Lip gestures describe visible mouth movements and do not identify emotions. To try expression mode, face the camera and hold a smile, rounded pout or pursed lips for a moment. Speaking can produce similar movements.
