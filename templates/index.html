A QUICK INTRODUCTION
Start with a moment.
1. Choose a visual source
Upload a video or enable your webcam. For a webcam capture, press Recognize capture. The optional live mode repeats short captures until you stop it.
2. Review the result
Unknown characters and empty output are flagged as unreliable. Even readable candidates require review. The public model is limited to GRID commands; the original model remains available for comparison. Neither is validated for general conversation.
3. Keep or delete your history
Results are private to your account and persist between visits. Export your results or delete them individually. Video files and camera images are not retained.
Camera troubleshooting
Use HTTPS when deployed, or localhost on your PC. Allow camera permission in the browser, close other apps using the camera, and keep this tab visible. If the camera is unavailable, use video upload.
About VisionSpeak
A visual speech recognition project using Python, TensorFlow, OpenCV, MediaPipe, Flask, HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No microphone, speech-to-text service or new model training is used.
